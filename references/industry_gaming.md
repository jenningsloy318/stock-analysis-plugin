# Gaming & iGaming Deep-Dive

## Industry Structure

### Value Chain Segments
| Segment | Description | Key Players | Moat Source |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Casino Operators (Land-Based)** | Physical casinos, resorts, hospitality | LVS, WYNN, MGM, CZR | Real estate, licenses, brand |
| **Online Sportsbooks** | Digital sports wagering platforms | DKNG, FLTR (FanDuel parent), BET (ESPN Bet), PENN | Market access, brand, user base |
| **iGaming Platforms** | Online casino games (slots, table games) | FLTR, Evolution (EVO), MGM (BetMGM), GAN | Content library, licenses, tech |
| **Gaming Equipment/Tech** | Slot machines, systems, table tech | IGT, LNW (Light & Wonder), AGS | Installed base, IP, casino relationships |
| **Sports Data/Content** | Odds feeds, data rights, streaming | SRAD (Sportradar), Genius Sports | Exclusive league partnerships |
| **Content Studios** | Game development (slots, live dealer) | EVO, Pragmatic Play, NetEnt (EVO) | Game portfolio, RNG certification |
| **Lottery** | State lotteries, instant tickets, iLottery | IGT, SGMS (now LNW), Pollard | Long-term state contracts, barriers to entry |

### Online Sports Betting (OSB) Market Dynamics
- **Handle**: Total amount wagered. Growing 20-40% YoY in US as new states legalize.
- **Hold rate**: % of handle retained as revenue. Structural 7-10% for sports, higher for parlays.
- **Parlay mix shift**: Higher-margin multi-leg bets growing as % of handle = structural hold improvement.
- **Same-game parlays (SGP)**: 15-25% hold rate vs 5-7% for single-game bets. Key margin driver.
- **In-play/live betting**: Growing from ~30% to 50%+ of handle (higher engagement, higher hold).

### iGaming vs Sports Betting Economics

| Dimension | Sports Betting | iGaming (Online Casino) |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------|
| **Gross Margin** | 25-40% (after free bets, taxes) | 40-60% (lower promotional intensity) |
| **Hold Rate** | 7-10% (structural) | 3-5% average (but 100% of sessions) |
| **Revenue per User** | $200-500/year | $500-1,500/year |
| **Customer Lifetime** | 2-4 years (churn to better odds) | 5-8 years (stickier, entertainment) |
| **Promotional Spend** | 30-50% of GGR (early state) → 15-20% (mature) | 15-25% of GGR (early) → 8-12% (mature) |
| **Regulatory Risk** | Moderate (widely accepted) | Higher (perceived social harm) |
| **TAM per Capita** | $150-300/adult (mature market) | $200-500/adult (mature market) |
| **State Legalization** | 38+ states legal (2026) | 7-8 states legal (slower adoption) |

## Key Gaming Metrics

### Land-Based Casino Operators

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **GGR (Gross Gaming Revenue)** | Wagers - Payouts (house win) | >5% YoY same-store growth | Negative same-store growth |
| **Hold Percentage** | GGR / Total handle (drop) | Stable or improving | Declining (competitive pricing) |
| **EBITDAR Margin** | EBITDAR / Net Revenue | >35% (Vegas Strip) >40% (regional) | <25% |
| **RevPAR (Hotel)** | Room revenue / Available rooms | >$200 (premium, Las Vegas) | Declining occupancy + ADR |
| **Slot Win per Unit per Day** | Daily slot GGR / # slot machines | >$300 (strong market) | <$150 (oversaturated) |
| **Table Drop per Table per Day** | Daily table game handle / # tables | >$5,000 | <$2,000 |
| **Capex / Revenue** | Maintenance + growth capex / Revenue | 5-10% (maintenance) | >20% without clear ROI |
| **Net Debt / EBITDAR** | (Total debt - Cash) / EBITDAR | <3.5x | >5.5x (over-leveraged) |

### Online Sportsbook / iGaming Operators

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Online Handle Growth** | YoY growth in total wagering volume | >25% (new state tailwind) | <10% (saturation) |
| **Hold Rate (Sportsbook)** | GGR / Handle | >9% (parlay mix helping) | <7% (adverse results, promo-heavy) |
| **GGR Growth** | YoY Gross Gaming Revenue growth | >30% (market expansion) | <10% |
| **Monthly Unique Players (MUPs)** | Distinct betting accounts active per month | >15% YoY growth | Flat or declining |
| **ARPDAU** | Average Revenue Per Daily Active User | >$3/day (strong engagement) | <$1/day (casual, low-value) |
| **DAU/MAU** | Daily Active / Monthly Active | >30% (high engagement) | <15% (low stickiness) |
| **Promotional Spend / GGR** | (Free bets + bonuses + odds boosts) / GGR | <20% (mature market) | >40% (unsustainable, market grab) |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | Total S&M / New depositing customers | <$300 (efficient) | >$600 (arms race) |
| **LTV/CAC** | Customer lifetime value / CAC | >3.0x | <1.5x (value destructive) |
| **State Contribution Margin** | State-level GGR - Direct costs - Promos - Tax | >20% (mature state) | Negative (new state, year 1-2) |

### Gaming Equipment & Technology

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Installed Base (Units)** | Total machines placed at casinos | Growing >3% YoY | Declining (replacement cycle weak) |
| **Replacement Cycle** | Average unit age at replacement | Shortening (new content demand) | Lengthening (capex cuts by casinos) |
| **Revenue per Unit per Day** | Participation/lease revenue / Installed units / Days | >$50/unit/day | <$30/unit/day |
| **Ship Share** | Units shipped / Total industry shipments | >25% (market leader) | <15% (losing share) |
| **Recurring Revenue %** | (Gaming operations + digital) / Total | >50% (stable, high-margin) | <30% (lumpy product sales) |
| **Game Performance** | Avg revenue per unit vs casino floor average | >1.2x floor average (premium content) | <0.9x (weak content pipeline) |

## Regulatory Framework

### State-by-State Legalization as Catalyst

| Regulatory Phase | Characteristics | Investment Implication |
|-----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Pre-legalization** | Legislative activity, ballot measures | Optionality value; monitor bill progress |
| **Legalization + licensing** | Law passes, RFP for licenses | Market access value; winners announced |
| **Launch (Year 1)** | Heavy promotional spending, customer acquisition | Revenue growth but margin-negative |
| **Maturation (Year 2-3)** | Promo rationalization, hold normalization | Margin inflection, contribution positive |
| **Steady State (Year 4+)** | Stable market share, rational competition | Normalized 25-35% EBITDA margins |

### Tax Rate Landscape (Critical for Margin Analysis)
| State/Jurisdiction | OSB Tax Rate | iGaming Tax Rate | Impact |
|--------------------|-------------|------------------|--------|
| **New York** | 51% | N/A (not legal) | Margin-crushing; only scale operators viable |
| **Pennsylvania** | 36% | 42% | High but viable at scale |
| **New Jersey** | 14.25% | 17.5% | Favorable; mature, profitable market |
| **Illinois** | 15-40% (graduated) | N/A | Moderate; largest recent opportunity |
| **Michigan** | 8.4% | 20-28% | Favorable OSB; moderate iGaming |
| **UK (comparison)** | 21% (point of consumption) | 21% | Mature benchmark for US maturity modeling |

### Regulatory Risk Assessment
- **Tax rate increases**: Legislators view gaming revenue as easy tax target. NY-style taxes spreading = margin pressure.
- **Advertising restrictions**: UK/Australia-style ad bans could raise CAC and reduce brand visibility.
- **Responsible gambling mandates**: Deposit limits, loss limits, mandatory breaks reduce revenue per user.
- **Sports league integrity fees**: Leagues pushing for 0.25-1% of handle as "integrity fee" — not yet enacted broadly.
- **Wire Act / federal intervention**: Low probability but existential risk to interstate operations.

## Market Maturity Curve

### Promotional Lifecycle Model
Track each state's position on the maturity curve:

| Phase | Promo Spend/GGR | Market Share Fluidity | EBITDA Margin | Duration |
|-------|----------------|----------------------|---------------|----------|
| **Land Grab (Year 1)** | 50-80% | High (switching common) | -30% to -10% | 12-18 months |
| **Rationalization (Year 2)** | 25-40% | Moderate (leaders emerging) | -5% to +10% | 12 months |
| **Maturation (Year 3)** | 15-25% | Low (top 3 control 70%+) | 15-25% | 12 months |
| **Steady State (Year 4+)** | 8-15% | Very Low (oligopoly) | 25-35% | Indefinite |

### TAM Expansion Modeling
For each new state legalization, estimate:
1. **Adult population** × online participation rate (15-25% for OSB, 10-15% for iGaming)
2. **Revenue per active user**: $300-500/year OSB, $600-1,200/year iGaming (based on mature states)
3. **Addressable GGR**: Population × participation × ARPU
4. **Market share assumption**: Top 2-3 operators capture 60-70% of market
5. **Timeline to steady state**: 3-4 years post-launch
6. **Tax and promo-adjusted contribution**: GGR × (1 - tax rate) × (1 - promo rate) × (1 - operating cost rate)

## Valuation Framework

### Key Multiples by Sub-Segment
| Sub-Segment | Primary Multiple | Premium Range | Fair Value | Discount Range |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|------------|----------------|
| **Land-Based Casino (Vegas)** | EV/EBITDAR | 12-16x | 9-12x | <8x |
| **Land-Based Casino (Regional)** | EV/EBITDAR | 9-12x | 7-9x | <6x |
| **Online Sportsbook (growth)** | EV/Revenue | 5-10x | 3-5x | <2x |
| **Online Sportsbook (mature)** | EV/EBITDA | 20-30x | 15-20x | <12x |
| **Gaming Equipment** | EV/EBITDA | 12-16x | 9-12x | <8x |
| **Sports Data/Content** | EV/Revenue | 8-15x (exclusive rights) | 5-8x | <4x |
| **Live Dealer/Content Studios** | EV/EBITDA | 20-30x (EVO premium) | 15-20x | <12x |

### Valuation Nuances
- **OpCo/PropCo splits**: Many casino operators separated real estate (REITs like VICI, GLPI) from operations. Value the OpCo on EBITDAR after rent.
- **State-level sum-of-parts**: Value each state license as a separate DCF based on its maturity phase.
- **Promotional spend normalization**: Adjust EBITDA for excess promotional spend above steady-state levels (add back 50% of above-normal promos).
- **Handle growth vs GGR growth**: Handle growth alone is misleading; hold rate normalization matters more for revenue quality.
- **New state optionality**: Unlegalized states (California, Texas, Florida for OSB) represent free call options on TAM expansion.

## Red Flags Specific to Gaming

- **Promotional spend/GGR rising in mature states**: Market share loss being offset by unsustainable promo spend
- **Hold rate consistently below structural average**: Poor risk management or adverse sports outcomes not normalizing
- **Customer acquisition cost inflating >20% YoY**: Marketing arms race destroying unit economics
- **Single-state revenue concentration >40%**: Regulatory risk if that state raises taxes or restricts operations
- **iGaming cannibalization of land-based**: Same-company online offering reducing brick-and-mortar visits without net revenue gain
- **Debt/EBITDAR >5x for land-based operators**: Over-leveraged in a cyclical discretionary spending business
- **Net deposits declining while handle grows**: Users recycling winnings rather than adding new funds (engagement without monetization)
- **Regulatory enforcement actions or license suspension**: Compliance failures signal operational risk
- **Heavy reliance on VIP/whale segment**: Single high-roller losses can materially impact quarterly results
- **Tax rate increases passed or proposed in key states**: Direct margin compression with no offset available
- **Management guiding to "market share investment" without LTV/CAC disclosure**: Value destruction masked as strategy
- **Sports data rights expiring without renewal certainty**: Loss of exclusive content = competitive disadvantage overnight
