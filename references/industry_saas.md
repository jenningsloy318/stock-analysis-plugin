# SaaS & Technology Deep-Dive

## Unit Economics Framework

### Core SaaS Metrics
| Metric | Formula | World-Class | Good | At Risk |
|--------|---------|-------------|------|---------|
| **NDR (Net Dollar Retention)** | (Beginning ARR + Expansion - Churn - Contraction) / Beginning ARR | >130% | 110-130% | <100% |
| **Gross Margin** | (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue | >80% | 70-80% | <65% |
| **Rule of 40** | Revenue Growth % + FCF Margin % | >50 | 30-50 | <20 |
| **CAC Payback (months)** | CAC / (Monthly Gross Margin per Customer) | <12 | 12-18 | >24 |
| **LTV / CAC** | (Gross Margin × Avg Lifetime) / CAC | >8x | 5-8x | <3x |
| **Magic Number** | (Current Quarter ARR - Prior Quarter ARR) × 4 / Prior Quarter S&M Spend | >1.0 | 0.7-1.0 | <0.5 |
| **RPO / Revenue** | Remaining Performance Obligations / Annual Revenue | >1.5x | 1.0-1.5x | <0.7x |

### Growth Efficiency Analysis
- **S&M efficiency**: New ARR / S&M spend → declining = market saturation or competition
- **Payback period trend**: Lengthening = worsening unit economics, shortening = improving GTM
- **Cohort analysis**: Revenue retention by vintage year — declining NDR in older cohorts = product rot
- **Logo vs wallet expansion**: % of growth from new logos vs existing customer expansion

### Competitive Moat in SaaS
| Moat Type | Signal | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Switching Costs** | Data gravity, workflow integration depth | NDR >120%, logo churn <5% |
| **Network Effects** | More users = more value | DAU/MAU >40% (consumer), NPS trending up |
| **Scale Economies** | Infrastructure cost per customer declining | Gross margin expanding YoY |
| **Data Network Effects** | More usage = better AI/product | Feature velocity accelerating |
| **Platform Lock-in** | API integrations, ecosystem breadth | 3rd-party app count, API call volume growth |

### TAM Expansion Assessment
- **Seat-based pricing**: TAM limited by employee count. Watch for seat growth deceleration.
- **Usage-based pricing**: TAM grows with customer success. More aligned but less predictable.
- **Platform expansion**: New product lines expanding TAM beyond initial category
- **International**: % revenue outside home market, localization investment

### Technology Stack Risk
- **Cloud concentration**: Single cloud provider risk (AWS/Azure/GCP)
- **Open source dependency**: % of codebase dependent on copyleft licenses
- **AI/ML moat**: Proprietary data vs commodity models — data advantage is durable
- **Platform risk (iOS/Android/Chrome)**: App Store revenue share, policy dependency

### Valuation Nuances
- **Revenue multiple vs growth rate**: Rule of thumb: EV/NTM Revenue ≈ Growth Rate × 0.5-1.0
- **The 40% Rule premium**: Companies sustaining Rule of 40+ for 3+ years justify 2-3x premium multiples
- **FCF conversion path**: Growth stage (-20% FCF margin) → Efficient growth (0% FCF margin) → Mature (+20% FCF margin)
- **SBC (Stock-Based Compensation)**: Dilution rate >3% annually is a red flag. Adjust FCF for SBC.
- **Acquisition integration risk**: Serial acquirer in SaaS → check product integration and churn post-acquisition

### Red Flags Specific to SaaS
- **Calculated billings declining while reported revenue growing**: Revenue recognition pulling forward
- **S&M spend growing faster than revenue**: GTM efficiency deteriorating
- **Customer count disclosed quarters ago, now only ARR**: Hiding logo churn
- **RPO growth decoupling from revenue growth**: Future revenue visibility declining
- **Switching from GAAP to non-GAAP primary reporting**: Masking deterioration
