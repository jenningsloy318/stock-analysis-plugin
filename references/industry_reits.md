## Real Estate / REITs — Sector-Specific Metrics & Valuation

### Key Sector Characteristics
- **GICS Sector**: 60 (Real Estate)
- **Primary valuation**: NAV (Net Asset Value), P/AFFO, Cap Rate, Dividend Yield
- **Why different**: REITs must distribute 90%+ of taxable income; depreciation distorts earnings; asset values diverge from book
- **Cycle sensitivity**: Interest rate sensitive (inverse to 10Y Treasury); construction cycle (supply vs demand)

### REIT-Specific Financial Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Healthy Range | Red Flag |
|--------|---------|---------------|----------|
| **FFO** | Net Income + Depreciation + Amortization - Gains on Sales | Growing | Declining |
| **AFFO** | FFO - Recurring Capex (maintenance) - Straight-line rent adj | >FFO*0.80 | AFFO/FFO < 0.70 |
| **AFFO Payout Ratio** | Dividends / AFFO | 60-80% | >90% (unsustainable) or <50% (cutting soon?) |
| **Same-Store NOI Growth** | YoY NOI growth excl. acquisitions/dispositions | >2% | Negative |
| **Occupancy Rate** | Occupied sqft / Total leasable sqft | >93% | <88% |
| **Weighted Avg Lease Term (WALT)** | Avg remaining lease duration weighted by revenue | >5yr (net lease) | <3yr (rollover risk) |
| **Debt/EBITDA** | Total Debt / EBITDA | 4-6x | >7x |
| **Interest Coverage** | EBITDA / Interest Expense | >3.0x | <2.0x |
| **Cap Rate** | NOI / Property Value | Sector-dependent | Compression vs expansion trend |
| **NAV Premium/Discount** | (Share Price - NAV per share) / NAV per share | ±10% | >30% premium or >25% discount |

### Valuation Methodology (DO NOT use standard P/E or DCF)

1. **NAV Approach** (primary): Sum property values at market cap rates, subtract net debt, divide by shares
2. **P/AFFO Multiple**: Compare to sub-sector median (Office: 14-18x, Industrial: 22-28x, Multifamily: 18-24x, Net Lease: 14-17x, Data Center: 25-35x, Cell Tower: 28-35x)
3. **Implied Cap Rate**: NOI / (Market Cap + Net Debt) — compare to private market transaction cap rates
4. **Dividend Discount**: AFFO growth + dividend yield = expected total return (should exceed cost of equity)

### Critical REIT Analysis Factors

1. **Balance sheet quality**: Fixed vs floating rate debt mix, weighted avg maturity, unencumbered asset ratio
2. **External vs internal growth**: Acquisition pipeline quality vs organic rent growth — external growth can mask weak fundamentals
3. **Lease structure**: Net lease (tenant pays opex) vs gross lease (landlord pays); escalator type (CPI-linked, fixed, percentage rent)
4. **Development pipeline**: % of portfolio under development, yield on cost vs market cap rate (spread = value creation)
5. **Tenant concentration**: Top-10 tenant revenue % (>30% is concentration risk); tenant credit ratings
6. **Geographic concentration**: Sunbelt exposure premium; gateway city discount
7. **Interest rate sensitivity**: Duration of assets vs liabilities; hedging effectiveness; floating rate exposure

### REIT Kill Switches
- AFFO payout ratio >100% for 2+ quarters
- Debt/EBITDA >8x with upcoming maturities
- Same-store NOI negative for 3+ quarters
- Dividend cut or suspension
- Major tenant bankruptcy representing >5% of revenue
- Occupancy drop below 85% (sub-sector dependent)
