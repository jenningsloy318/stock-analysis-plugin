# Banking & Financials Deep-Dive

## Loan Book Analysis

### Credit Quality Metrics
| Metric | Strong | Adequate | Weak | Critical |
|--------|--------|----------|------|----------|
| Net Charge-Off Rate | <0.25% | 0.25-0.50% | 0.50-1.0% | >1.0% |
| Non-Performing Loans / Total Loans | <1.0% | 1.0-2.0% | 2.0-4.0% | >4.0% |
| Allowance for Credit Losses (ACL) / NPLs | >150% | 100-150% | 75-100% | <75% |
| CECL Reserve / Total Loans | >2.0% | 1.5-2.0% | 1.0-1.5% | <1.0% |

### Loan Portfolio Composition
- **Commercial vs Consumer split**: Consumer = more stable, Commercial = cyclical
- **CRE (Commercial Real Estate) exposure**: Office retail risk post-pandemic — flag >15% of total loans
- **C&I (Commercial & Industrial)**: Cyclical, credit-line heavy
- **Residential mortgage**: Rate-sensitive, prepayment risk
- **Credit card / Auto**: Higher yield, higher loss content
- **Geographic concentration**: Single-state banks carry regional economic risk

### Net Interest Margin (NIM) Analysis
- **Rate sensitivity**: Asset-sensitive (benefits from rising rates) vs Liability-sensitive
- **Deposit beta**: % of rate hikes passed to depositors — lower beta = better NIM in rising cycle
- **Non-interest-bearing deposits**: % of total deposits — declining = funding cost pressure
- **Loan yield vs deposit cost spread**: Track quarterly trend

### Capital Adequacy
- **CET1 Ratio**: >11% = well-capitalized, <7% = undercapitalized
- **Tier 1 Leverage Ratio**: >8% = strong, <4% = weak
- **Total Risk-Based Capital Ratio**: >13% = strong
- **Stress test results (CCAR)**: Capital return capacity after severe adverse scenario

### Efficiency & Profitability
- **Efficiency Ratio** (Non-Interest Expense / Revenue): <55% = excellent, >65% = bloated
- **ROTCE** (Return on Tangible Common Equity): >15% = best-in-class, <8% = value-destroying
- **ROA**: >1.2% = strong, <0.6% = weak
- **Fee income % of total revenue**: Higher = less rate-sensitive, but watch regulatory risk

### Key Risks
- **Credit cycle position**: Late-cycle = tighten underwriting, early-cycle = growth opportunity
- **Interest rate risk**: Duration gap analysis, EVE (Economic Value of Equity) sensitivity
- **Liquidity**: LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio), NSFR, wholesale funding dependence
- **Regulatory**: Basel III Endgame, stress test requirements, GSIB surcharge
- **FinTech disruption**: Digital banking, BNPL, embedded finance threat assessment

### Valuation Specifics
- **P/B vs ROTCE**: P/B should scale with ROTCE. Rule of thumb: P/B = 1 + (ROTCE - 10%) × 10
- **P/TBV** (Tangible Book Value): More relevant than P/B for acquisitive banks (goodwill adjusted)
- **Dividend yield + buyback yield**: Total shareholder return yield
- **Pre-provision net revenue (PPNR)**: Operating profitability before credit costs
