# Communication Services Deep-Dive

## Industry Structure

### Sub-Sectors
| Segment | Description | Key Players | Revenue Model |
|---------|------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Interactive Media** | Social platforms, search, content aggregation | META, GOOGL, SNAP, PINS, RDDT | Advertising (~80-98%) |
| **Entertainment/Streaming** | Video streaming, gaming, music | NFLX, DIS, SPOT, WBD, PARA | Subscription + advertising |

## Key Interactive Media Metrics

| Metric | Formula/Definition | World-Class | Good | At Risk |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|------|---------|
| **DAU/MAU Ratio** | Daily Active Users / Monthly Active Users | >50% (communication), >30% (content) | 30-50% | <20% |
| **ARPU (Global)** | Average Revenue Per User / quarter | Growing >10% YoY | Growing 5-10% | Declining |
| **Engagement (Time Spent)** | Average minutes per user per day | >45 min/day | 25-45 min/day | <15 min/day |
| **Ad Revenue Growth** | YoY ad revenue change | >20% | 10-20% | <5% |
| **Ad Load** | Ads per user session | Stable to declining (pricing-driven growth) | Slightly growing | Growing >10% YoY (user fatigue) |
| **CPM/CPC Trend** | Cost per thousand impressions / Cost per click | Growing >8% YoY | Growing 3-8% | Declining |
| **Operating Margin** | Operating Income / Revenue | >35% | 25-35% | <20% |

## Key Streaming/Entertainment Metrics

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Target |
|--------|-------------------|--------|
| **Subscribers** | Total paying subscribers by region | Growth >10% YoY (mature), >25% (growth markets) |
| **ARPU** | Monthly revenue / Average subscribers | Growing YoY (price increases, ad tier upside) |
| **Churn Rate** | Monthly cancellations / Beginning subscribers | <3%/month (excellent), <5% (good), >7% (concerning) |
| **Content Spend / Revenue** | Amortized content cost / Revenue | 40-55% (sustainable), >60% (inefficient spend) |
| **Content ROI** | Subscriber adds per $1M content spend | Declining = content saturation |
| **Engagement Hours** | Total viewing hours / Subscriber / Month | Growing or stable |
| **FCF Margin** | FCF / Revenue | Positive and expanding (key inflection point) |

### Streaming Profitability Benchmarks
| Maturity Stage | Revenue Growth | Op Margin | FCF | Key Sign |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|-----|---------|
| Investment | >20% | Negative | Deeply negative | Subscriber growth justifies investment |
| Scaling | 15-20% | Improving toward breakeven | Approaching breakeven | Content spend efficiency improves |
| Mature | 5-10% | 15-20%+ | Strongly positive | Capital returns begin (buybacks) |

## Competitive Moat in Communication Services

| Moat Type | Signal | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Network Effects** | Direct (more users = more value), cross-side (advertiser ↔ user), data (more usage → better AI/targeting) | DAU growth + ARPU growth simultaneously |
| **Switching Costs** | Social graph, content library, carrier contract (device payment plan lock-in) | Churn rate, NPS, portability |
| **Scale Economies** | Content cost amortized over more subs, infrastructure cost per GB declining | Margin expansion with scale |
| **Intangible Assets** | Brand (must-have status for advertisers), spectrum licenses (barrier to entry), IP library (Disney, Warner Bros) | Pricing power, renewal rates |
| **Regulatory Moat** | Spectrum licenses (limited resource), local franchise agreements, must-carry rules | Spectrum depth (MHz/POP), franchise renewal |

## Advertising Market Analysis

### Macro Sensitivity
The advertising market is highly cyclical — the most GDP-sensitive revenue stream in the S&P 500:
- **Recession beta**: ~2-3x GDP decline (ad budgets are first cut, last restored)
- **Leading indicator**: Ad agency guidance and upfront commitments lead overall market by 3-6 months
- **Recovery pattern**: Digital ads recover first (performance-based), then brand/linear

### Structural Share Shifts
| Channel | Share Trend | Drivers |
|---------|------------|---------|
| Search | Stable/Growing | AI overviews may cannibalize some queries but expand total search volume |
| Social Video (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) | Growing rapidly | Engagement shift from static to video, better monetization than feed |
| Connected TV (CTV) | Growing | Cord-cutting + programmatic ad buying |
| Linear TV | Declining 5-10%/yr | Cord-cutting, aging demo, measurement challenges |
| Retail Media (Amazon/Walmart ads) | Growing fastest | First-party purchase data, closed-loop attribution |
| Audio/Podcast | Growing | Programmatic audio buying, podcast ad-tech improving |

### Key Advertising KPIs by Channel
1. **Search**: Query volume growth, CPC, ad coverage (paid clicks / total queries)
2. **Social**: Ad impressions, CPM, ad load %, advertiser count growth, SMB vs enterprise split
3. **CTV/Streaming**: Ad-tier subscriber %, ad load per hour, CPM vs linear TV
4. **Retail Media**: Ad revenue as % of GMV, advertiser count, take rate

## Red Flags Specific to Communication Services

### Interactive Media / Social
- **User growth decoupling from revenue growth**: Revenue up but DAU flat = monetization saturation
- **Ad load increasing while engagement time declining**: User experience deterioration
- **Creator flight**: Top creators reducing posting frequency or migrating platforms
- **Regulatory risk (Section 230, KOSA, EU DSA/DMA)**: Platform liability, algorithmic transparency, data portability requirements
- **Apple ATT / privacy deprecation drag**: Revenue impact from reduced targeting capability

### Streaming / Entertainment
- **Content spend growing faster than revenue**: Negative operating leverage
- **Subscriber growth from lower-ARPU regions**: Mix shift reducing blended ARPU
- **Password sharing crackdown boost is one-time**: Growth rate normalizes post-crackdown
- **Library depth but declining engagement**: Content volume doesn't equal viewer engagement
- **Sports rights cost escalation**: NFL/NBA/Premier League rights growing >50% per renewal cycle

### Telecom
- **Wireless ARPU declining in competitive promotions**: Price war signal
- **Capex/revenue >18% without corresponding revenue growth**: Overbuilding
- **Lead in copper retirement timetable**: Stranded legacy cost risk
- **Fixed Wireless Access cannibalizing fiber ROI**: Lower-ARPU technology undercutting premium product
- **Device subsidy battle escalating**: Higher SAC (Subscriber Acquisition Cost) eroding margins
