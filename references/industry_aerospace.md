# Aerospace & Defense Deep-Dive

## Industry Structure

### Value Chain Segments
| Segment | Description | Key Players | Cycle Sensitivity |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Primes** | System integrators, platform OEMs | LMT, RTX, NOC, GD, BA (defense) | Low (long-cycle, backlog-driven) |
| **Tier 1 Suppliers** | Major subsystems (engines, avionics, sensors) | RTX (P&W, Collins), GE Aero, L3Harris, HII | Low-Moderate (locked into programs) |
| **Tier 2/3 Suppliers** | Components, structures, machined parts | HEICO, TDG, SPR, HXL | Moderate (delivery rate dependent) |
| **MRO (Maintenance, Repair, Overhaul)** | Aftermarket services, spare parts | AAR Corp, ST Engineering, OEM MRO divisions | Low (fleet utilization driven) |
| **Services & IT** | Defense IT, cyber, mission systems | LDOS, BAH, CACI, SAIC | Very Low (recurring, cost-plus) |
| **Space** | Launch, satellites, ground systems | LMT (Space), NOC (Space), RKLB, ASTS | Moderate (government + commercial) |
| **Commercial Aerospace** | Airframes, engines, interiors | BA, EADSY (Airbus), SPR, TDG | High (airline cycle, delivery rates) |

### Program Lifecycle Stages
Understanding where a program sits in its lifecycle is critical for margin/revenue forecasting:

| Phase | Duration | Revenue Profile | Margin Profile | Cash Profile |
|-------|----------|----------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Development (EMD)** | 3-7 years | Low (cost reimbursable) | Low/Negative (investment) | Cash burn |
| **LRIP (Low Rate Initial Production)** | 2-4 years | Moderate (ramp) | Below mature (learning curve) | Breakeven |
| **FRP (Full Rate Production)** | 5-15 years | Peak volume | Peak margin (scale + learning) | Strong cash generation |
| **Sustainment/Modernization** | 10-30+ years | Declining volume, steady services | Highest margin (spare parts, upgrades) | Excellent FCF |

### Classification Impact on Analysis
- **Classified programs** (black programs) reduce disclosure: revenue recognized but no program-level detail
- **Classified backlog** may be 10-30% of total for primes like NOC and LMT
- **Analyst estimates are less reliable** for companies with high classified mix
- **Key indicator**: Watch for "restricted" or "classified" revenue growth commentary in earnings calls

## Key Aerospace & Defense Metrics

### Defense (Primes & Major Subs)

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Backlog / Revenue** | Total backlog / Annual revenue | >3.0x (multi-year visibility) | <1.5x (booking drought) |
| **Book-to-Bill** | Orders received / Revenue recognized | >1.1x (backlog growing) | <0.9x (backlog eroding) |
| **Program Win Rate** | Major competitions won / Total competed | >40% | <25% (losing share) |
| **Aftermarket / Recurring %** | (MRO + Services + Spare Parts) / Total Revenue | >40% (high-quality earnings) | <20% (production-dependent) |
| **Government / Commercial Mix** | Government revenue / Total | 60-80% (stable base) | >95% (no diversification) |
| **International %** | Non-domestic revenue / Total | >25% (diversified, FMS growth) | <10% (domestic-only concentration) |
| **Op Margin** | Operating income / Revenue | >12% (prime), >15% (services) | <8% (cost overruns, low-margin dev) |
| **FCF Conversion** | FCF / Net Income | >100% (asset-light aftermarket) | <70% (heavy capex/working capital) |
| **R&D / Revenue (company-funded)** | IRAD + B&P / Revenue | >3% (investing in next-gen) | <1.5% (living off legacy) |

### Margin by Contract Type

| Contract Type | Typical Margin | Risk Profile | Key Metric |
|---------------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| **Cost-Plus** | 8-12% (award fee driven) | Low (cost passthrough) | Award fee scores |
| **Fixed-Price Development** | 0-8% (often negative in early lots) | Very High (overrun risk) | EAC (Estimate at Completion) revisions |
| **Fixed-Price Production** | 10-15% (learning curve benefit) | Moderate | Unit cost reduction rate |
| **T&M (Time & Materials)** | 8-12% | Low | Billable headcount utilization |
| **IDIQ/Task Order** | 10-14% | Low | Task order win rate, ceiling utilization |

### Commercial Aerospace

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Delivery Rate** | Aircraft delivered per month | Increasing (rate ramp) | Decreasing or below plan |
| **Backlog (years)** | Total orders / Annual deliveries | >7 years (demand secured) | <4 years (order drought) |
| **ASP (Average Selling Price)** | Revenue per aircraft delivery | Rising (mix shift to widebody) | Declining (discounting) |
| **OEM Aftermarket Growth** | Spare parts + services revenue growth | >8% YoY (fleet aging, utilization) | <3% (fleet grounding, new fleet) |
| **Narrowbody/Widebody Mix** | Revenue split NB vs WB | WB share growing (higher margin) | Narrowbody only (commoditized) |
| **RPK Recovery (vs 2019)** | Revenue Passenger Kilometers vs baseline | >100% of 2019 (structural growth) | <90% (demand impaired) |
| **Engine Shop Visits** | Annual overhaul/repair events | Growing with fleet + utilization | Declining (new fleet displacing) |

### Tier 2/3 Suppliers & Aftermarket

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish Threshold | Bearish Threshold |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Organic Revenue Growth** | Revenue growth excl. M&A | >8% (rate ramps + aftermarket) | <3% (mature programs winding down) |
| **EBITDA Margin** | EBITDA / Revenue | >25% (aftermarket-heavy, TDG model) | <12% (structures/commodity) |
| **Sole-Source %** | Revenue from sole-source positions | >70% (pricing power) | <40% (competitive bid exposure) |
| **Installed Base Growth** | Fleet count on platforms served | Growing (new deliveries + fleet aging) | Shrinking (retirements > deliveries) |
| **PMA Exposure** | Revenue at risk from PMA parts competition | <10% of aftermarket | >25% (FAA PMA approvals eroding moat) |

## Defense Budget Cycle Analysis

### Budget Framework
| Component | Description | Analysis Approach |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **FYDP (Future Years Defense Program)** | 5-year DoD spending plan | Identifies program funding trajectory |
| **NDAA (Authorization)** | Annual defense policy bill | Sets policy/priorities, not funding |
| **Appropriations** | Actual funding allocation | The only number that matters for revenue |
| **Continuing Resolution (CR)** | Temporary funding at prior-year levels | Delays new starts, limits growth |
| **Sequestration** | Automatic across-the-board cuts (BCA) | Rare but devastating if triggered |

### Budget Cycle Impact
| Scenario | Impact on Primes | Impact on Suppliers | Duration |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------| ---------|
| **Budget growth >3% real** | New program starts, accelerated procurement | Supply chain ramp, hiring | Sustained 3-5 years typically |
| **Flat budget (0-3% real)** | Prioritization trade-offs, program stretch-outs | Stable but no upside | Can persist for years |
| **Continuing Resolution** | No new starts, flat spending, uncertainty | Delayed orders, inventory build | Typically 1-6 months |
| **Budget decline / sequestration** | Program cuts, stretch-outs, cancellations | Rate reductions, destocking | Recovery takes 2-3 years |

### Current Cycle Position Assessment
Key questions to answer:
1. What is the bipartisan appetite for defense spending? (China/Russia threat narrative)
2. Where are we in the CR vs full-year appropriation cycle?
3. Which modernization priorities (nuclear triad, space, cyber, hypersonics) are bipartisan protected?
4. What is the supplemental funding outlook? (Ukraine, Indo-Pacific, Israel)

## Long-Cycle Dynamics

### Program Lock-In Economics
- **Development phase**: 5-10 years of R&D creates irreplaceable institutional knowledge
- **Production sole-source**: Once in production, switching costs are prohibitive (recertification takes years)
- **Sustainment lock-in**: OEM controls technical data, spare parts IP, depot maintenance capability
- **Typical program duration**: 30-50 years from development start to fleet retirement
- **Switching cost example**: Replacing an engine on a fighter program would cost $5-10B+ and take 10+ years

### Backlog Quality Assessment
Not all backlog is equal:
| Backlog Type | Quality | Revenue Visibility |
|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Funded backlog** | Highest — appropriated money allocated | >95% conversion within 12-24 months |
| **Unfunded backlog (obligated)** | High — contract in place, awaiting funding | >80% conversion within 36 months |
| **IDIQ ceiling** | Moderate — maximum potential value | 40-70% conversion over contract life |
| **Options/Future lots** | Lower — expected but not guaranteed | 60-80% for production options |
| **International pipeline** | Variable — subject to FMS/regulatory approval | 30-60% depending on country/program |

## Competitive Moat in Aerospace & Defense

| Moat Type | Signal | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Sole-Source Lock-In** | Proprietary designs on long-cycle programs | % revenue from sole/single-source contracts |
| **Installed Base** | Large fleet requiring OEM-specific sustainment | Fleet size × remaining service life |
| **Security Clearance** | Classified program access, SCIF facilities | Cleared employee count, facility clearance level |
| **Technical Complexity** | Few competitors capable of system integration | # of qualified bidders on recent competitions |
| **Regulatory Barriers** | ITAR, export controls, nuclear certification | Barriers to non-US and non-cleared competitors |
| **Customer Relationships** | Decades of program history with DoD/allied militaries | Customer retention rate, follow-on awards |
| **Vertical Integration** | Control of critical subsystems/components | Make vs buy ratio on key subsystems |

## Valuation Framework

### Key Multiples
| Sub-Segment | Primary Multiple | Premium Range | Fair Value | Discount Range |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|------------|----------------|
| **Primes** | P/E (NTM) | >20x (growing backlog + margin expansion) | 16-20x | <14x (budget uncertainty) |
| **Defense Services/IT** | EV/EBITDA | >15x (organic growth + margins) | 12-15x | <10x |
| **Commercial Aero OEM** | P/E (mid-cycle) | >25x (rate ramp) | 18-25x | <15x (production issues) |
| **Aftermarket/Parts (TDG model)** | EV/EBITDA | >20x (sole-source + pricing power) | 15-20x | <12x |
| **Tier 2/3 Suppliers** | EV/EBITDA | >14x (sole-source, aftermarket) | 10-14x | <8x (commodity structures) |
| **Space (pure-play)** | EV/Revenue | >5x (early-stage, high growth) | 3-5x | <2x (execution concerns) |

### Valuation Nuances
- **Peak margin trap**: Defense primes at peak FRP margins on major programs — margin compression ahead as programs transition to sustainment or new dev programs win
- **Development drag**: Companies winning large EMD contracts see near-term margin pressure but long-term franchise value
- **Pension adjustments**: Legacy primes (RTX, BA, LMT) carry significant pension obligations — check funded status
- **Goodwill from M&A**: Defense consolidation created large goodwill balances — verify acquisition-driven ROIC

## Red Flags Specific to Aerospace & Defense

- **Book-to-bill <0.9 for 3+ consecutive quarters**: Backlog erosion, market share loss
- **Fixed-price development EAC increases**: Cost overruns consuming margin (e.g., BA defense programs)
- **Customer concentration >50% from single program**: Program cancellation = revenue cliff
- **Workforce attrition in cleared personnel**: Cannot easily replace TS/SCI-cleared engineers
- **International offset obligations >30% of contract value**: Forced technology transfer and local production erode long-term advantage
- **Subcontractor delivery delays cascading to prime schedule**: Supply chain risk (castings, forgings, electronics)
- **Pension underfunding worsening**: Rising discount rate sensitivity, potential cash contributions
- **No next-generation program in pipeline**: Current programs aging out without franchise replacement
- **FMS sales >40% of backlog in politically unstable regions**: Execution risk, cancellation, sanctions
- **Commercial aero supplier with single-platform dependency**: 737 MAX or A320neo rate changes = outsized impact
