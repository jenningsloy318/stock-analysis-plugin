# Energy Deep-Dive

## Industry Structure

### Sub-Sectors
| Segment | Description | Business Model | Key Drivers |
|---------|------------|----------------|-------------|
| **E&P (Upstream)** | Oil & gas exploration and production | Commodity price × volume - costs | WTI/Brent, production growth, F&D costs |
| **Integrated Majors** | Upstream + downstream + chemicals | Diversified across value chain | Portfolio balance, energy transition strategy |
| **Midstream** | Pipelines, storage, transport | Fee-based, volume-sensitive | Utilization, contracted vs spot exposure |
| **Refining** | Crude processing into products | Crack spread (margin) × throughput | Crack spreads, utilization, turnaround schedule |
| **Oilfield Services** | Drilling, completion, production services | Activity × pricing | Rig count, service intensity, pricing power |
| **Renewables** | Wind, solar, storage, hydrogen | PPA-based, subsidy-driven | Capacity additions, power prices, IRA subsidies |
| **LNG** | Liquefaction, shipping, regasification | Long-term contracts + spot | Global gas spreads (HH vs TTF vs JKM) |

## Key Energy Metrics

### Upstream (E&P / Integrated)

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|-------------------|---------|---------|
| **Reserve Replacement Ratio** | New reserves added / Annual production | >150% | <100% |
| **F&D Cost/BOE** | Finding & Development costs / Proved reserves added | <$15/BOE | >$30/BOE |
| **Lifting Cost/BOE** | Production operating expense / BOE produced | <$10/BOE | >$20/BOE |
| **Breakeven WTI** | WTI price at which FCF = 0 (covers capex + dividend) | <$45/bbl | >$65/bbl |
| **Debt/EBITDA** | Total debt / EBITDA | <1.5x | >3.0x |
| **FCF Yield** | FCF / Market Cap | >10% | <5% |
| **Production Growth** | YoY volume growth | 3-8% (sustainable) | >15% (aggressive) or negative |
| **Proved Developed %** | PD reserves / Total proved reserves | >70% | <50% |
| **Recycle Ratio** | Operating cash flow per BOE / F&D cost per BOE | >2.0x | <1.0x |

### Midstream

| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Contract Coverage** | >85% fee-based/take-or-pay | <60% or high spot exposure |
| **Leverage (Debt/EBITDA)** | <4.0x | >5.5x |
| **Distribution Coverage** | DCF/Distribution >1.2x | <1.0x (unsustainable) |
| **Volume Growth** | >3% throughput growth | Declining throughput |
| **Counterparty Quality** | >80% investment-grade customers | Customer bankruptcy risk |

### Refining

| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Crack Spread ($/bbl)** | >$20/bbl (WTI 3-2-1) | <$10/bbl |
| **Utilization Rate** | >95% | <85% |
| **Capture Rate** | >90% of benchmark crack | <70% |
| **Turnaround Schedule** | No major turnarounds next 6 months | Major unit downtime scheduled |
| **Renewable Diesel %** | Growing from base | No transition investment |

### Renewables / Clean Energy

| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Capacity Additions (GW)** | Accelerating YoY | Decelerating or delayed |
| **PPA Price ($/MWh)** | Above $40/MWh | Below $25/MWh (competitive) |
| **PPA Duration** | >15 years weighted avg | <10 years |
| **Developer Margin** | >20% project-level IRR | <8% unlevered IRR |
| **Interconnection Queue** | Projects advancing to COD | Stuck in queue >3 years |
| **Equipment Cost Trend** | Module/turbine costs declining | Rising (tariffs, supply chain) |

## Commodity Price Framework

### Oil Supply/Demand Balance
- **OPEC+ spare capacity**: Below 3 mb/d = tight. Above 5 mb/d = glut risk.
- **US production growth**: 0-500 kb/d/year is normal. >1 mb/d = oversupply risk.
- **Global demand growth**: 0.5-1.5 mb/d/year is normal. Below 0 = recession signal.
- **Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR)**: Releases = bearish for prices. Refills = bullish floor.

### Natural Gas
- **Storage vs 5-year average**: Below avg heading into winter = bullish. Above avg in spring = bearish.
- **LNG export capacity growth**: Each new train adds 1-2 Bcf/d of demand pull.
- **Coal-to-gas switching**: Gas below $3/MMBtu triggers power sector switching.

## Energy Transition Analysis

### Transition Risk Assessment
For each company, assess:
1. **Scope 1+2 emissions intensity** (tCO2e/$M revenue) vs sector median
2. **Low-carbon capex %**: Share of capex going to renewables, hydrogen, CCS, biofuels
3. **Reserve life vs demand peak**: Years of proved reserves at current production vs IEA NZE scenario
4. **Carbon price sensitivity**: Impact of $50/tCO2 and $100/tCO2 on unit cash costs
5. **Asset stranding risk**: Which assets would be uneconomic in a rapid transition scenario?

### Transition Opportunity
1. **LNG as transition fuel**: Companies with LNG exposure benefit from coal-to-gas switching
2. **Carbon capture (CCS)**: Differentiator for companies with sequestration assets (pore space, Class VI wells)
3. **Blue/Green hydrogen**: Companies with gas + CCS or renewable + electrolyzer projects
4. **Biofuels/Renewable diesel**: Refining conversion capability, feedstock sourcing advantage

## Competitive Moat in Energy

| Moat Type | Signal | Most Applicable To |
|-----------|--------|---------------------|
| **Resource Quality** | Low-cost basin access, high-quality rock | E&P |
| **Infrastructure Scale** | Pipeline network, terminal access, irreplaceable ROW | Midstream |
| **Technology/Process** | Complex refining capability (heavy sour crude processing) | Refining |
| **Portfolio Diversification** | Multi-basin, multi-commodity, integrated value chain | Majors |
| **Cost Position** | Bottom-quartile breakeven on global cost curve | E&P, OFS |
| **Contract Structure** | Long-term take-or-pay contracts with creditworthy counterparties | Midstream, LNG |

## Red Flags Specific to Energy

- **Reserve replacement <100% for 3+ years**: Liquidating, not sustaining
- **F&D costs rising >15% YoY**: Basin exhaustion or poor capital allocation
- **Production growth funded by debt**: Production up, but Debt/EBITDA rising = growth at any cost
- **Hedging book locking in below-market prices**: If >70% of production hedged below strip, upside capped
- **Permian/Eagle Ford concentration risk**: Single-basin E&Ps carry geological + regulatory concentration
- **Unfunded decommissioning liabilities (ARO)**: Check asset retirement obligations vs market cap on older offshore/US assets
- **Renewables companies with negative FCF + rising rates**: Utility-scale solar/wind developers are highly rate-sensitive; FCF yield must exceed 10Y Treasury + 200bp risk premium
