# Insurance Deep-Dive

## Industry Structure

### Sub-Sectors
| Segment | Description | Key Drivers | Valuation Metric |
|---------|------------|-------------|------------------|
| **P&C (Property & Casualty)** | Auto, home, commercial property, liability | Pricing cycle (hard/soft market), catastrophes, reserve adequacy | P/B, Combined Ratio |
| **Life Insurance** | Term life, whole life, annuities, long-term care | Interest rates, mortality trends, policyholder behavior | P/B, EV (Embedded Value) |
| **Health Insurance** | Employer, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid MCO, individual | Medical cost trend, enrollment, regulatory (ACA) | P/E, MLR |
| **Reinsurance** | Catastrophe reinsurance, specialty, life reinsurance | Pricing cycle, cat losses, alternative capital inflows | P/B, Combined Ratio |
| **Insurance Brokers** | Commercial, benefits, reinsurance brokerage | Organic growth, margin expansion, M&A | P/E, EV/EBITDA |
| **Mortgage/Financial Guaranty** | PMI, municipal bond insurance | Housing cycle, credit trends, persistency | P/B, ROE |

## Key P&C Insurance Metrics

| Metric | Formula/Definition | Industry Benchmark | Red Flag |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|----------|
| **Combined Ratio** | (Losses + Expenses) / Premiums | 95-100% (sub-95 is excellent) | >105% |
| **Loss Ratio** | Incurred Losses / Earned Premiums | 60-70% (depends on line) | >80% (reserve deficiency risk) |
| **Expense Ratio** | Underwriting Expenses / Written Premiums | 25-35% | >40% (inefficient) |
| **Reserve Development** | Prior-year reserve releases as % of beginning reserves | +0 to +3% (favorable development) | Unfavorable >2% of equity |
| **Net Written Premium Growth** | YoY NWP change | 5-12% (hard market can be 15-25%) | Negative in hard market |
| **Investment Yield** | Net Investment Income / Average Invested Assets | 3-5% | Below risk-free rate |
| **ROE** | Net Income / Equity | 8-12% (target) | <5% over multiple years |
| **BVPS Growth** | Book Value Per Share growth | 5-10%+ | Negative (destructive) |
| **P/B Ratio** | Price / Book Value | 1.0-2.0x (quality-dependent) | <0.7x (distress signal or value opportunity) |
| **Float / Market Cap** | Insurance float / Market Cap | N/A — higher = more investment leverage | Float declining |

### Line-of-Business Specific

**Personal Auto:**
| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Rate Adequacy | Rate increases > loss cost trend | Rate trails loss cost inflation |
| Frequency Trends | Declining (ADAS, safer cars) | Rising (distracted driving, repair costs) |
| Severity Trends | Inflation-matching | Outpacing CPI by >300bp |

**Commercial Property:**
| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Pricing (RMI) | +10%+ rate increases | Flat to down |
| Catastrophe Load | Adequate cat budget relative to PML | Exceeding cat budget 2 of last 3 years |
| Reinsurance Cost | Stable or declining risk-adjusted | Spiking post-cat event |

**Cyber Insurance:**
| Metric | Bullish | Bearish |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Premium Growth | >25% YoY (rapidly growing market) | Slowing sharply |
| Loss Ratio | <60% (profitable) | >80% (ransomware surge) |
| Aggregation Risk | Well-modeled systemic events | Unmodeled systemic exposure |

## Key Life Insurance Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **ROE (Statutory)** | Statutory Net Income / Statutory Surplus | 8-12% |
| **Risk-Based Capital (RBC)** | Regulatory capital / Risk-weighted assets | >400% (well-capitalized) |
| **Spread Income** | Investment portfolio yield - Crediting rate to policyholders | >150bp |
| **Embedded Value (EV)** | PV of future profits + adjusted net worth | Growing >5% YoY |
| **EV / Share** | Embedded Value per share | Best valuation metric for life insurers |
| **New Business Margin** | Value of New Business / PV of New Business Premiums | >3% (Asia), >1.5% (US) |
| **Lapse Rate** | Policies surrendered / Average policies in force | <5% annually (lower is better) |
| **Interest Rate Sensitivity** | EV impact per ±100bp rate move | Disclose and manage. Large negative sensitivity = risk. |

### Key Risks for Life Insurers
- **Interest rate gap**: Long-duration liabilities (30yr+ guarantees) vs shorter-duration assets
- **Policyholder behavior risk**: Mass lapses in rising-rate environments, accelerated utilization of living benefits
- **Credit migration**: Corporate bond portfolio downgrades → higher capital requirements
- **Longevity risk**: Pension risk transfer (PRT) blocks exposed to longer-than-expected lifespans
- **Private equity/alternative asset exposure**: Growing as % of general account — liquidity mismatch risk
- **Regulatory**: FASB LDTI (Long-Duration Targeted Improvements) changes earnings patterns; Bermuda/US statutory differences

## Key Health Insurance Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Medical Loss Ratio (MLR)** | Medical Claims / Premiums | 80-85% (individual/small group), 85%+ (large group) |
| **Enrollment Growth** | YoY membership growth | 3-8% (organic) |
| **Premium Yield** | Average premium per member | Rising with medical cost trend |
| **Medical Cost Trend** | YoY change in per-member medical costs | 4-7% (normal range) |
| **Days Claims Payable (DCP)** | Claims reserves / Daily claims expense | 45-60 days |
| **Quality Stars (Medicare Advantage)** | CMS star rating (1-5) | 4+ stars (mandatory for bonus payments) |

### ACA / Regulatory Risk
- **Subsidy dependency**: Enhanced subsidies (ARP/IRA) expire 2025 without Congressional extension → enrollment cliff risk
- **Medicare Advantage rate notice**: Annual CMS rate update sets revenue for ~50% of large health insurers' earnings
- **Medicaid redeterminations**: Continuous enrollment unwinding → membership headwind for Medicaid MCOs

## Reinsurance Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Combined Ratio** | (Loss + Expense) / Premiums | <95% (long-term average) |
| **ROE** | Net Income / Equity | 10-15% (through the cycle) |
| **Premium Growth** | YoY NWP growth | 5-10% organic |
| **Alternative Capital** | ILS/cat bond market size | Growing = lower reinsurance pricing |
| **1-in-250 PML / Equity** | Probable Maximum Loss (250yr) / Shareholders' Equity | <20% (comfortable), >30% (elevated) |
| **Catastrophe Budget Exhaustion** | Actual cat losses / Catastrophe budget | <100% average over 5yr cycle |

## Competitive Moat in Insurance

| Moat Type | Signal | Measurement |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Underwriting Discipline** | Consistently sub-100 combined ratio across cycles | 10-year average combined ratio |
| **Distribution Network** | Exclusive/captive agency force, digital DTC capability | Cost per policy acquired, retention rate |
| **Data & Pricing** | Proprietary risk models, telematics data, claims analytics | Loss ratio vs peers in same lines |
| **Float Quality** | Low-cost, long-duration, sticky liabilities | Float growth rate, cost of float |
| **Scale in Specialty Lines** | Market share in niche lines (E&S, cyber, D&O) | Top-3 position in 3+ specialty lines |
| **Regulatory Capital Efficiency** | Low RBC ratio with high ratings (efficient use of capital) | RBC >300% with AM Best "A" or higher |
| **Reinsurance Access** | Strong relationships, favorable terms, exclusive facilities | Multi-year reinsurance treaties, panel diversification |
| **Claims Management** | Proprietary claims data, litigation management, fraud detection | Claims closure rate, defense cost ratio |

## Cycle Analysis

### The P&C Pricing Cycle (Hard vs Soft Market)
| Phase | Combined Ratio | NWP Growth | Capital Position | Action |
|-------|---------------|------------|-----------------|--------|
| **Hard Market** | Improving (↓) | High (+15-25%) | Capital constrained | Buy — pricing power strongest |
| **Hard → Transition** | <95% | Decelerating | Capital rebuilding | Hold — peak pricing rolling off |
| **Soft Market** | Rising (↑) | Low (<5%) | Excess capital | Avoid — price competition eroding margins |
| **Soft → Transition** | >100% | Stabilizing | Capital depleted | Monitor — watch for rate inflection |

### Cycle Indicators to Monitor
1. **Industry combined ratio**: Above 100% for 2+ quarters → hard market approaching
2. **Reinsurance pricing**: YoY rate-on-line change at January 1 renewals
3. **Catastrophe losses**: Above-average cat year depletes capital → hard market
4. **New entrants / MGAs**: Proliferation of startups → capital abundant → soft market
5. **Reserve releases**: Industry reserving adequacy declining → early sign of under-reserving

## Red Flags Specific to Insurance

- **Reserve deficiency**: Adverse development >5% of equity in any year
- **Aggressive reserve releases**: Releases consistently >5% of earnings = masking underwriting losses
- **Investment portfolio risk**: Below-investment-grade bonds >10% of portfolio, commercial mortgage exposure, private credit
- **Reinsurance recoverable risk**: Large reinsurance recoverables from low-rated or concentrated reinsurers
- **Regulatory action**: Risk-based capital below company action level, AM Best downgrade review
- **Climate/catastrophe concentration**: >30% of premiums in peak cat zones (FL wind, CA earthquake/ wildfire)
- **Growth through acquisition**: Serial acquirer in insurance → integration risk, reserve surprises, goodwill buildup
- **Annuity/longevity tail risk**: Life insurers with large legacy blocks where assumptions are stale (mortality improvement, lapse rates)
- **Alternative capital in reinsurance**: ILS/cat bond market >$100B has structurally suppressed catastrophe pricing
