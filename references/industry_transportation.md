# Industry Deep-Dive: Transportation & Logistics

## Sector Context

Transportation spans airlines, railroads, trucking, shipping (container/bulk/tanker), logistics/freight forwarding, and ride-sharing/delivery. These are cyclical businesses with high operating leverage, asset-intensive balance sheets, and strong sensitivity to fuel costs and trade volumes. Railroads are the exception — they're quasi-monopolies with pricing power.

## Valuation Methodology

### Airlines: EV/EBITDAR (Lease-Adjusted)

- Airlines must be valued on lease-adjusted metrics (operating leases = debt equivalents)
- EV/EBITDAR: 4-6x for legacy, 6-8x for LCC with growth
- NEVER use P/E alone (earnings are hyper-cyclical)
- Mid-cycle earnings assumption required for fair value

### Railroads: P/E and EV/EBITDA (Premium Multiple Justified)

- Railroads are infrastructure monopolies — deserve premium
- P/E: 18-25x (justified by pricing power + capital return)
- EV/EBITDA: 12-16x
- FCF yield: 4-6% target
- Compare operating ratio trends, not absolute levels

### Trucking: EV/EBITDA with Cycle Adjustment

- LTL (oligopoly): 10-14x EV/EBITDA
- TL (competitive): 6-9x EV/EBITDA
- Adjust for cycle position (freight recession vs boom)

### Shipping: P/NAV (Asset-Based)

- Primary: Price/NAV (fleet liquidation value)
- P/NAV < 0.7 = deep value (if balance sheet allows)
- P/NAV > 1.3 = expensive (unless newbuild replacement cost higher)
- Secondary: EV/EBITDA at mid-cycle charter rates (NOT current spot)

## Critical Risk Factors

1. **Fuel price spike**: Airlines and trucking have high fuel sensitivity
2. **Recession/trade war**: Volume collapse hits asset-heavy operators hard
3. **Overcapacity cycles**: Shipping and airlines chronically over-order in booms
4. **Labor**: Pilot shortages, driver turnover, port strikes
5. **Regulatory**: Emissions (IMO 2023 for shipping, SAF mandates for airlines), cabotage rules
6. **Geopolitical**: Red Sea/Suez disruption, US-China trade decoupling, sanctions

## Kill Switches

- Operating ratio deteriorating >300bps YoY with no volume recovery in sight
- Fuel exceeds 35% of airline OpEx without hedging coverage
- Shipping orderbook exceeds 25% of fleet (oversupply tsunami incoming)
- Railroad precision-scheduled railroading (PSR) implementation causing service failures and customer defection
- Freight recession enters 3rd consecutive quarter with spot rates below breakeven
- Net Debt/EBITDAR exceeds 5.0x (airlines) or 4.0x (non-airlines)
