# Biotech & Pharma Deep-Dive

## Clinical Trial Phase Analysis

### Phase Transition Probabilities
| Phase | Success Rate | Typical Duration | Key Risks |
|-------|-------------|------------------|-----------|
| Preclinical → Phase 1 | 10% | 1-2 years | Toxicology, formulation |
| Phase 1 → Phase 2 | 52% | 2 years | Safety, dosing |
| Phase 2 → Phase 3 | 29% | 3 years | Efficacy signal, endpoint selection |
| Phase 3 → NDA/BLA | 58% | 3-4 years | Statistical significance, safety database |
| NDA/BLA → Approval | 85% | 10-12 months | FDA review, advisory committee |

### rNPV Calculation
```
rNPV = Σ (Peak Sales × Market Share × Probability of Success × Patent-Life-Adjusted Discount)
```
- Discount rate: 10-12% for early-stage, 8-10% for late-stage
- Peak sales estimate: epidemiology × pricing × penetration
- Patent life remaining after approval = key driver

### FDA Calendar Tracking
- **PDUFA dates**: FDA decision deadlines — monitor closely
- **Advisory Committee (AdCom) meetings**: 1-3 months before PDUFA
- **sNDA/sBLA**: Supplemental approvals for label expansion
- **CRL (Complete Response Letter)**: Rejection — identify root cause and remediation timeline

### Patent Cliff Analysis
- **Exclusivity loss exposure**: % of revenue from products losing patent within 5 years
- **Patent expiration dates**: Track per-product
- **Biosimilar / generic entry timeline**: First-to-file, 180-day exclusivity
- **Patent litigation status**: IPR (Inter Partes Review) challenges, settlements (pay-for-delay)

### Pipeline Depth Score (1-10)
| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | 3+ Phase 3 assets, 5+ Phase 2, no cliff >30% revenue in 5yr |
| 7-8 | 2+ Phase 3, 3+ Phase 2, cliff <20% |
| 5-6 | 1 Phase 3, pipeline thin, cliff <30% |
| 3-4 | No Phase 3, heavy reliance on 1-2 products |
| 1-2 | Patent cliff imminent, no pipeline offset |

### R&D Productivity
- **rNPV / Cumulative R&D spend** (10-year): >1.5x = excellent, <1.0x = value-destroying
- **R&D as % of revenue**: Compare to peer median
- **Internal vs acquired pipeline**: Acquired assets carry premium
- **Platform value**: Technology platform generating multiple assets (e.g., mRNA, CRISPR) → premium

### Key Metrics
- Cash runway: (Cash + Equivalents) / Quarterly Net Burn → <8 quarters = dilution risk
- Revenue concentration: % from top 3 products → >60% = concentration risk
- Gross-to-net: List price vs net price spread (rebates, discounts)
- Drug pricing risk: IRA negotiation exposure, international reference pricing

### Approval Catalysts
| Catalyst Type | Typical Stock Impact | Probability |
|--------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Positive Phase 3 data | +30-100% | 58% |
| FDA Approval | +15-30% | 85% |

| Label expansion approval | +5-15% | 80% |
| Patent upheld in court | +5-20% | Varies |
